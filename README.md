## 🚀 What I'm Working On

🎮 **Project NULL** 

A small-scope sci-fi delivery game about isolation, risk, and consequences.

You operate from inside a tiny ship.

Pilot. Dock. Deliver.  
Then deal with whatever the void throws at you.

• physically interact with cockpit controls  
• short, constrained flights — no open-world bloat  
• handcrafted anomalies & random events  
• every decision costs money, hull, or sanity  
• tight scope, high tension

Designed to prove that strong systems and constraints create better gameplay than scale.

Minimal space. Maximum design.
